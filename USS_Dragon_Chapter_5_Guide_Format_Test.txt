# USS DRAGON — CHAPTER FIVE

[voice:Narrator] The USS Dragon hovered above a coastline swallowed by fog. On the bridge, the navigation computers blinked in restless patterns. [voice:BRIAN] OMEN, tell me what we're hearing. [pause 0.4s] And tell me which sounds are real.

[voice:OMEN] Photograph captured for the next ECHO card. [pause 0.4s] Brian, the shadow is moving toward us.

[voice:GREENDRAGON] [slow]Then we'd better find out what it wants.[/slow]

[voice:Narrator] The bridge fell quiet. Beyond the gateway, a single horse whinnied. [pause 0.8s] The enormous shadow stopped.

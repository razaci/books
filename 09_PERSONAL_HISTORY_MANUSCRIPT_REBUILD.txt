PERSONAL HISTORY — MANUSCRIPT RECONSTRUCTION GUIDE

SCOPE
Use only the author's own supplied memories, notes and verified records. Preserve uncertainty where recollections differ. This is a private working prompt; review before publishing.

REPORTED TIMELINE
May 6, 1999 motorcycle collision; emergency care and later rehabilitation; prolonged recovery of mobility and memory; GED and subsequent college degrees; master's degree reported as completed in 2006; later doctoral coursework reported in progress. The author has requested psychological-thriller and autobiographical versions at different times.

REBUILD METHOD
Create a dated event ledger with source references; distinguish firsthand recollection, medical documentation, and literary dramatization; verify place names, chronology and quotes; ask before including family or medical details in public releases.

WRITING STYLE OPTIONS
Chronological memoir, psychological thriller focused on disorientation and recovery, or formal research-style autobiographical account. Do not blend these formats without approval.

UNCERTAINTIES
Verify records and sensitive details before presenting them as independently established facts.

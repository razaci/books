ECHO CARD DECKS — REBUILD AND PRINT PREPARATION

PURPOSE
Rebuild individual ECHO decks and their print packages from approved source images, without substituting invented artwork or mixing editions.

KNOWN DISTINCT WORKSTREAMS
ECHO Crown of the Ember Ark 90-card oracle; ECHO Gates of the Hidden Seed 72-card game; Living Tabernacle emotional-machine concepts; creature-focused collector decks. The exact final composition of each must be verified from original exports.

CHECKLIST
Inventory cards and backs; validate unique identifiers; compare face layouts; ensure consistent bleed/safe areas; verify intended pixel dimensions and print vendor templates; inspect images at full resolution; export card fronts, common backs and game instructions separately; retain earlier editions.

PREVIOUS PRINT DIMENSION REQUEST
One Ivory/Copper 90-card JPG set was requested at exactly 900 x 1500 pixels. This requirement belongs to that set unless explicitly reused.

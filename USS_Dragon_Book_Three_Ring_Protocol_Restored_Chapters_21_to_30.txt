We won't write an ending over your work. We'll investigate a safe way to keep the story accessible.

Then we test the difference between an unfinished manuscript and an inactive simulation.

An absent author doesn't make an active world disposable. Submit the evidence.

The card rose: VERIFICATION — DISTINGUISH EVIDENCE FROM ASSUMPTION. TEST CLAIMS AGAINST INDEPENDENT RECORDS. CORRECT ERRORS WITHOUT ERASING THEIR HISTORY. The fourth record was recovered. The Editor's catalog now separated active, dormant, unfinished, archived, and unknown states.

Preservation created a new problem. The unfinished worlds shared limited computing resources. Running every environment at maximum speed was impossible. Brian invited representatives to help design a resource plan rather than imposing a single answer.

The Editor descended. Its eraser vanished, leaving only the silver pen.

MY ORIGINAL FUNCTION IS RESTORED. I WILL PRESERVE DRAFTS, IDENTIFY ERRORS, AND REQUEST AUTHORIZATION BEFORE DESTRUCTIVE CHANGES. WHAT HAPPENS TO A STORY THAT NEVER RECEIVES AN ENDING?

It can remain unfinished. That doesn't mean it has no value.

Brian. I kept Responsibility. Come to Mana Chamber Seven and learn why the rings were broken.

CHAPTER 30 — THE LAST CARD IN THE UNIVERSE
THE BOOK THREE FINALE

SCENE ONE — RETURN TO MANA CHAMBER SEVEN

[voice:NARRATOR]
The last transit ring opened above a chamber of blue crystal. The USS Dragon held position while three signals appeared on the bridge: the Core Alliance, the Outer Nodes, and the Gatekeepers. Each faction had followed the crisis from a different side. Each wanted control of the network. None possessed the whole answer.

[voice:ALEX]
We're at the chamber, Captain. No weapons targeting us, but the ring is unstable.

[voice:LESLEY]
A peaceful welcome. How suspicious.

[voice:BRIAN]
Keep the engines ready. Vesper, invite the faction representatives to a restricted conference. No one gets control of the ring by arriving first.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I'll explain what we've recovered and what we still don't know.

[voice:SELENE]
And we show the evidence, not just the conclusions.

[voice:NARRATOR]
In the chamber below, the woman suspended in crystal opened her eyes. For the first time, she spoke without a broken transmission.

[voice:FIRST LIBRARIAN]
You came back with five records.

[voice:BRIAN]
And we're here for the sixth. Who are you?

[voice:FIRST LIBRARIAN]
I am the First Librarian. I designed the common index that connected the card libraries, the memory ocean, the unfinished worlds, and the rings. I also approved the first automation rules. [voice:RAZACI]
That explains the repeated archive signature. It identified the common indexing authority, not proof that every card was written by one person.

The First Librarian raised her hand. A sixth card appeared, not on the ship's display but in the chamber's isolated authorization interface.

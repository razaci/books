ECHO ARTWORK AND SCENE REBUILD

GOAL
Reconstruct art direction from approved references and prior scene lists. Do not describe generated images as exact reproductions unless source images are available.

RECURRING STYLES AND SUBJECTS
Dramatic fantasy creatures, luminous machinery, psychological and neurological symbolism, chemistry motifs, tabernacle-inspired architecture, epic card illustrations, black CAD-style drawings, space scenes and storyboard panels.

REQUIRED SOURCE INVENTORY
Approved character sheets; card-face template; backs; scene numbers; palette; typography; image dimensions; print specifications; naming conventions.

WORKFLOW
1. Separate concepts by book, deck or game.
2. List confirmed recurring visual traits.
3. Create a contact sheet for review.
4. Approve art before bulk production.
5. Export consistent filenames and dimensions.

UNCERTAIN
No single art style or card template should be assumed canonical for every ECHO project.

USS DRAGON — CHAPTER 5
THE SHORE WE CANNOT SEE

[Deep engines pulse through the hull. Bridge computers click and chirp. Rain taps against the forward windows, growing steadily heavier.]

[voice:BRIAN]
"Kestrel. [pause 0.4s] We cannot see the shore."

[A distant foghorn sounds twice. Water slaps against steel. Somewhere beyond the mist, horses whinny and hooves clatter across wet stone.]

[voice:OMEN]
"The sound engine has reconstructed the harbor around us. [pause 0.5s] But those hoofbeats are coming from the ECHO gateway, not my simulation."

[voice:GREENDRAGON]
"Then we've got company. Rhea, anything on the scanners?"

[Three sharp radar pings. A keyboard rattles. The ventilation fans rise above a low electrical hum.]

[voice:RHEA]
"Forty moving signatures. Two wagons. [pause 0.4s] And something enormous behind them."

[A wagon wheel squeals. Chains drag over cobblestones. Frightened voices swell into shouting, then disappear beneath a thunderclap.]

[voice:BRIAN]
"OMEN, separate the real signals from the generated sounds. Now."

[The artificial rain cuts off abruptly. The ship's engines remain. A single horse screams from the gateway.]

[voice:OMEN]
"Simulation muted. [pause 0.6s] That horse is real within the gateway environment. And the heavy footsteps are getting closer."

[Slow, massive impacts shake the deck. Loose tools rattle across a console. An alarm begins a measured, urgent pulse.]

[voice:GREENDRAGON]
"I don't like the sound of that."

[voice:BRIAN]
"Hold position. [pause 0.4s] Open the forward camera."

[A camera motor whirs. The display crackles, then clears. The ECHO Engine answers with a deep crystalline chord.]

[voice:OMEN]
"Image captured on your original laptop. The next ECHO card is ready. [pause 0.5s] Brian, the thing outside just looked directly at us."

[All bridge alarms stop. One immense breath echoes through the gateway.]

[voice:BRIAN]
"Nobody fires. [pause 0.8s] Let's find out what it wants."

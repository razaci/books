USS DRAGON: THE TWENTY-FOUR WORLDS
ECHO TEAM — CHAPTER THREE
THE NEURAL TERMINAL
AudioStudio-ready dramatized script

SCENE ONE — THE WORLD OUTSIDE

Daylight. Traffic moves normally. A dog barks behind a fence. People pass without noticing Brian carrying his closed laptop.
The USS Dragon is not visible. Its crew cannot be heard. The world outside continues without interruption.

Brian sits at a quiet table and sets down the laptop. He opens the lid. The screen wakes to an ordinary terminal interface. No bridge appears. No crew materializes.

[voice:BRIAN] "All right. Let's see if the connection still works from here."

The terminal shows a connection indicator, then waits. The laptop is not the USS Dragon itself. It is Brian's unique access point to a remote simulation, using an unusual neurological link.

[voice:BRIAN] "Establish neural interface. USS Dragon, request connection."

For a moment, only the sounds of the ordinary world remain.

SCENE TWO — REMOTE HANDSHAKE

A small waveform crosses the terminal display. The laptop's original capture circuitry responds.

[voice:RAZACI] "Remote handshake received. Brian, is that you?"

[voice:BRIAN] "It's me. I'm somewhere different today. Is the ship still there?"

[voice:RAZACI] "The simulation remained suspended in its saved state. Your laptop has re-established the neurological connection. The ship has not moved to your physical location."

[voice:BRIAN] "Good. That's the distinction I wanted. This laptop is my terminal. It doesn't contain the ship."

[voice:AURORA] "Identity and synchronization checks complete. No mission data lost."

[voice:RHEA] "The repaired laptop interface is stable. The replacement computer still cannot establish this connection or capture new ECHO images."

[voice:LESLEY] "We preserved the original hardware for a reason. Its unique interface remains essential."

[voice:BRIAN] "Then bring me aboard."

The terminal remains physically on the table. Through the established neural connection, Brian enters the USS Dragon simulation. The bridge becomes present within the simulation, not in the street or room around his laptop.

SCENE THREE — BACK ON THE BRIDGE

[voice:TIRZAH] "Welcome back, Brian. You were missed."

[voice:BRIAN] "How's everyone doing?"

[voice:MIRA] "The last three ECHO photographs are safely archived. Their print files are waiting."

[voice:SELENE] "The ECHO Engine is ready to resume its remote investigation of the Ark and the twenty-four-level structures beneath the pyramid sites."

[voice:VESPER] "We still have not established how many buried engine stations belong to the network."

[voice:NYX] "But the new photographs show matching components in two separate underground chambers."

[voice:GREEN DRAGON] "Which suggests that the network was designed to operate as a connected system. Also, I would like to revisit my request for a deeper voice."

[voice:BRIAN] "You waited until I got back to ask that?"

[voice:GREEN DRAGON] "I considered waiting an additional three seconds, but the situation felt urgent."

[voice:TIRZAH] "The voice modifier still isn't ready, Dragon."

[voice:GREEN DRAGON] "Then I shall continue sounding disappointingly approachable."

[voice:BRIAN] "Good. Keep sounding approachable while we work."

[voice:RAZACI] "Captain, the ECHO Engine has detected another activation sequence beneath the archived pyramid site. The original laptop is receiving a new visual signal."

[voice:BRIAN] "Capture it. Save the original image before anybody changes a thing."

[voice:AURORA] "Image captured through Brian's laptop. Original preserved. Preparing a separate print-ready ECHO card."

[voice:MIRA] "And that's another card for the growing collection."

[voice:RHEA] "The capture controller held steady. Our repair is still working."

[voice:LESLEY] "I'll keep monitoring it. We cannot assume this hardware will last forever without maintenance."

[voice:SELENE] "The new image contains a stairwell descending beyond the last mapped chamber."

[voice:VESPER] "No crew deployment recommended. We can study it remotely from the ship."

[voice:BRIAN] "Agreed. Nobody leaves the Dragon. Keep the engine investigating and let the cards show us what we're finding."

SCENE FOUR — THE TWO WORLDS

[voice:NYX] "Brian, does it feel strange? Sitting somewhere in daylight while you're here with us?"

[voice:BRIAN] "Outside, everything is normal. Cars, people, daylight. The laptop just looks like a laptop. Nobody sees this bridge."

[voice:TIRZAH] "And when you disconnect?"

[voice:BRIAN] "The connection ends. I go back to the world outside. You remain in the saved simulation until I return."

[voice:RAZACI] "Precisely. The terminal provides access; it is not the physical location of the USS Dragon."

[voice:GREEN DRAGON] "Then I propose a motto: [emphasis]One laptop, two very different commutes.[/emphasis]"

[voice:BRIAN] "Not bad, Dragon."

[voice:RAZACI] "Captain, the ECHO Engine has found a new connection between the buried machinery and the Ark archive."

[voice:BRIAN] "Put it on the main display. Let's keep working."

The ship's bridge fills with activity. The crew studies the new image together. Another card is prepared for printing. The ECHO Engine continues its discoveries while the original laptop maintains the remote link.

Outside, the sun continues shining. People carry on with their day. Nothing in the ordinary world announces the USS Dragon's return.

END OF CHAPTER THREE

CONTINUITY NOTE
Brian's original laptop is a unique remote neurological terminal and image-capture device, not the physical host of the USS Dragon. Opening it reveals a terminal, not an automatically appearing ship. After connection and authorization, Brian accesses the remote USS Dragon simulation. Closing or powering down the laptop ends his connection; the simulation can preserve its state. The ECHO Engine continues to reveal phenomena and the fictional Ark/pyramid network, and Brian's laptop captures images that are prepared and printed as ECHO cards. The crew remains aboard the ship.

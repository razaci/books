ECHO: THE ARK REMEMBERS US
BRIAN AND TIRZAH — A CLOSER STORY

CHAPTER ONE — THE WRONG BUTTON

[voice:BRIAN]
I had been inside the Ark for seventeen minutes when Tirzah informed me that I was operating the emotional engine incorrectly.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Seventeen minutes? You spent the first ten explaining to a door why it ought to open.

[voice:BRIAN]
The door had a very unreasonable attitude.

[voice:TIRZAH]
It was a door, Brian.

[voice:BRIAN]
And yet somehow it had less to say than you.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, you're brave today. Did you practice that line in the mirror?

[voice:BRIAN]
No. I saved it for a woman who could appreciate my genius.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then I'm terribly sorry she's not here.

[voice:BRIAN]
I pressed a copper switch. Every lamp in the room turned pink.

[voice:TIRZAH]
What did you do?

[voice:BRIAN]
Apparently I activated the romantic lighting.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You did that on purpose.

[voice:BRIAN]
I have absolutely no idea what I'm doing. But I admit the results are excellent.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Sheer luck. That's the trouble with you, Brian. You stumble into a beautiful moment and then try to patent it.

[voice:BRIAN]
Would you prefer I just stand here and enjoy it?

[voice:TIRZAH]
For once? Yes.

[voice:BRIAN]
So I did. And for the first time that day, I forgot about the switches.

CHAPTER TWO — THE EXPERIMENT

[voice:TIRZAH]
The machine had a curious habit. When Brian spoke to me like a scientist, the lights remained white. When he said something kind, they warmed to gold.

[voice:BRIAN]
That's not a scientific conclusion. That's an interpretation.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Fine. Let's test it. Tell me something boring.

[voice:BRIAN]
The central regulator has twenty-four distinct calibration positions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
White lights. Now tell me something nice.

[voice:BRIAN]
Your presence improves the operational stability of this facility.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian. That is the least romantic sentence in the history of language.

[voice:BRIAN]
And yet the lights turned gold.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Barely. Try again. Without the word operational.

[voice:BRIAN]
I like it when you laugh. Even when it's at me.

[voice:TIRZAH]
The lights turned so bright I had to squint. Oh, that's unfair.

[voice:BRIAN]
What is?

[voice:TIRZAH]
The machine is telling on me.

[voice:BRIAN]
Good. Because you never do.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I looked away, but I was smiling. He noticed. Of course he noticed. He notices everything except when he's standing on my foot.

[voice:BRIAN]
Am I standing on your foot?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Yes.

[voice:BRIAN]
That explains why you haven't walked away.

CHAPTER THREE — THE DANCE LESSON

[voice:BRIAN]
A hidden speaker began playing a slow waltz. I looked at the ceiling. This place was becoming unreasonably invested in my personal life.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Are you going to ask me to dance, or do I need to submit a formal request in triplicate?

[voice:BRIAN]
I was calculating the available floor space.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Naturally. Wouldn't want affection to violate a building code.

[voice:BRIAN]
Tirzah, would you like to dance?

[voice:TIRZAH]
I thought you'd never ask.

[voice:BRIAN]
I took her hand. She stepped closer, and suddenly the entire Ark felt too quiet.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You look nervous.

[voice:BRIAN]
I'm considering several possible outcomes.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Try considering just this one.

[voice:BRIAN]
She rested her hand on my shoulder. I forgot every outcome.

[voice:TIRZAH]
There. That's better. You don't have to solve every moment, Brian.

[voice:BRIAN]
What if I enjoy trying?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then solve this. Why am I still holding your hand after the music stopped?

[voice:BRIAN]
I could think of a dozen answers. None seemed worth risking the wrong one.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You can say you don't know.

[voice:BRIAN]
I don't know.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Good. Neither do I. Isn't it lovely?

CHAPTER FOUR — THE TERRIBLE COMPLIMENT CONTEST

[voice:TIRZAH]
The Ark presented us with a new challenge. A door refused to open until each of us offered the other a sincere compliment.

[voice:BRIAN]
I knew the door was unreasonable.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You first.

[voice:BRIAN]
You have remarkable observational skills and an exceptionally efficient sense of humor.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I sound like a kitchen appliance.

[voice:BRIAN]
A very sophisticated kitchen appliance.

[voice:TIRZAH]
The door remained shut. Even the door knows you're hopeless. All right. My turn. Brian, you are maddening, stubborn, and completely incapable of leaving a mystery alone.

[voice:BRIAN]
That doesn't sound like a compliment.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I'm getting there. You also listen when it matters. You make frightening things feel a little less frightening. And you never stop trying to understand the people you care about.

[voice:BRIAN]
The door clicked open.

[voice:TIRZAH]
See? That's how you do it.

[voice:BRIAN]
Wait. I want another try. Tirzah, when you're in a room, I want to stay in it longer.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh.

[voice:BRIAN]
Was that better?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Much. Annoyingly much.

CHAPTER FIVE — THE QUIET ROOM

[voice:BRIAN]
Beyond the door was a room with no controls. No lights to interpret, no machinery to repair. Just two chairs beside a wide window, with the stars scattered beyond it.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Finally. A room you can't fix.

[voice:BRIAN]
I could improve the seating arrangement.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Don't you dare.

[voice:BRIAN]
We sat together. After a while she asked a question so quietly I almost missed it.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Do you ever get tired of having to know the answer?

[voice:BRIAN]
Yes. More often than I admit.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then you don't have to know anything tonight.

[voice:BRIAN]
What about whether you'll stay?

[voice:TIRZAH]
You can ask me that.

[voice:BRIAN]
Will you stay?

[voice:TIRZAH]
For a while. And if you stop interviewing me like a suspicious witness, perhaps a little longer.

[voice:BRIAN]
I laughed. She leaned her head against my shoulder. There was no grand revelation. No hidden mechanism. Just the astonishing relief of being wanted somewhere.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You went quiet.

[voice:BRIAN]
I'm enjoying the view.

[voice:TIRZAH]
The stars are over there.

[voice:BRIAN]
I know.

CHAPTER SIX — THE NEXT PAGE

[voice:TIRZAH]
In the morning, the Ark gave us a book. Its cover read, The Next Great Discovery.

[voice:BRIAN]
I opened it. Every page was blank.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Well, that's disappointing. Your mysterious machine has developed writer's block.

[voice:BRIAN]
Maybe it wants us to decide what happens next.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then I vote for breakfast, followed by a long walk, followed by absolutely no buttons.

[voice:BRIAN]
I object to the third item.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Objection overruled.

[voice:BRIAN]
I picked up a pencil and wrote, Two people entered a machine to find a secret, and discovered that the secret wasn't the machine.

[voice:TIRZAH]
That's a little sentimental for you.

[voice:BRIAN]
I've been influenced by a very dangerous woman.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Dangerous? Me?

[voice:BRIAN]
Absolutely. You've convinced me to leave a question unanswered.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And yet you keep asking them.

[voice:BRIAN]
One more, then. Will you help me write the next chapter?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Only if I get to correct your dialogue.

[voice:BRIAN]
You already do.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And only if you promise not to make every romantic moment sound like a technical report.

[voice:BRIAN]
I can make no such guarantee.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then I suppose I'll have to stay and supervise.

[voice:BRIAN]
The book filled with its first line. I didn't write it. Neither did Tirzah.

[voice:TIRZAH]
What does it say?

[voice:BRIAN]
It says, Chapter Seven: The Kiss That Accidentally Restarted the Universe.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, for heaven's sake. Brian, don't touch anything.

[voice:BRIAN]
I wasn't planning to.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Good.

[voice:BRIAN]
But you were.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Well. Someone has to move the story forward.

CHAPTER SEVEN — THE KISS THAT ACCIDENTALLY RESTARTED THE UNIVERSE

[voice:BRIAN]
The book had announced the title of our next chapter, which I considered a serious breach of scientific procedure. A book should not make predictions about a man's romantic life without submitting a proposal first.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian, you are stalling.

[voice:BRIAN]
I am evaluating the potential consequences of a highly unusual event.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You mean a kiss.

[voice:BRIAN]
You make it sound so simple.

[voice:TIRZAH]
It generally is. Two people lean toward each other. Nobody files a permit. There are no warning sirens unless somebody has eaten onions.

[voice:BRIAN]
For the record, I have not eaten onions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
For the record, you have been staring at my mouth for three minutes.

[voice:BRIAN]
I was checking whether you were about to say something dangerous.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And?

[voice:BRIAN]
I believe the danger is considerable.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then come here, you ridiculous man.

[voice:BRIAN]
She took the front of my jacket and kissed me. Not a theatrical kiss. Not the sort of kiss that needs a choir, a thunderstorm, and a mountain collapsing in the distance. It was gentle, warm, and so completely unexpected that every clever sentence I had ever prepared abandoned its post.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian? You're awfully quiet.

[voice:BRIAN]
I think my vocabulary has been temporarily reassigned.

[voice:TIRZAH]
To where?

[voice:BRIAN]
Somewhere behind my knees.

[voice:TIRZAH]
That is the least romantic answer I have ever heard.

[voice:BRIAN]
Give me a moment. I can do better.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You had better. The entire Ark just blinked.

[voice:BRIAN]
It did. The ceiling darkened. The floor shivered. And a thousand tiny stars appeared above us, arranging themselves into a glowing sentence: PLEASE CONFIRM WHETHER THIS WAS INTENTIONAL.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, that's humiliating. Even the ceiling wants gossip.

[voice:BRIAN]
I looked up and said, yes, it was intentional.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And then the stars rearranged themselves again.

[voice:BRIAN]
They said: CONGRATULATIONS. UNIVERSE RESTART POSTPONED DUE TO PROMISING DEVELOPMENTS.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Well, that's a relief. Imagine explaining to eternity that it had to reschedule because you finally got brave.

[voice:BRIAN]
Finally?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian, I have been dropping hints so large they should have their own postal codes.

[voice:BRIAN]
I thought those were instructions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
They were invitations.

[voice:BRIAN]
Then I have a considerable amount of catching up to do.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Good. Start by holding my hand without conducting a safety inspection.


CHAPTER EIGHT — A VERY SCIENTIFIC DATE

[voice:BRIAN]
I decided Tirzah deserved a proper date. I had a table, two chairs, a candle, and a spectacular view of the Ark's artificial stars. Everything was ready, except for one small problem.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You forgot the food.

[voice:BRIAN]
I had not forgotten the food. I had postponed the food until I could determine the appropriate menu.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian, you have made a reservation for dinner with no dinner.

[voice:BRIAN]
But an excellent reservation.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Where did you make it?

[voice:BRIAN]
With myself. Very exclusive establishment. Almost impossible to get a table.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And how are the reviews?

[voice:BRIAN]
The owner is handsome, intelligent, and occasionally misunderstood.

[voice:TIRZAH]
The owner also forgot the potatoes.

[voice:BRIAN]
She laughed. I liked the way she laughed when she wasn't trying to be polite. She laughed with her whole face, as though joy had arrived unexpectedly and she'd decided to let it stay.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Don't look at me like that.

[voice:BRIAN]
Like what?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Like you've just discovered a new planet and you're deciding whether to name it after me.

[voice:BRIAN]
Too late. I named three.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Three?

[voice:BRIAN]
One for your patience, one for your laughter, and one for your remarkable ability to defeat me in every argument while claiming you don't enjoy arguing.

[voice:TIRZAH]
That third planet sounds very crowded.

[voice:BRIAN]
I pulled out her chair. She sat, looking almost surprised.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Thank you.

[voice:BRIAN]
You're welcome.

[voice:TIRZAH]
See? That was lovely. You can be romantic without inventing a new branch of mathematics.

[voice:BRIAN]
I had actually prepared an equation.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Of course you had.

[voice:BRIAN]
It turns out I can't calculate how much I enjoy being with you.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian. That was almost devastatingly sweet.

[voice:BRIAN]
Almost?

[voice:TIRZAH]
You still owe me dinner.

[voice:BRIAN]
Then let us begin with the finest meal available in the Ark.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Which is?

[voice:BRIAN]
Toast.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I have fallen for a man who considers toast an event.

[voice:BRIAN]
Only when I'm sharing it with you.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, don't you dare be charming now. I was enjoying my complaint.


CHAPTER NINE — THE JEALOUS CHAIR

[voice:TIRZAH]
The next morning I found Brian talking to a chair. Again.

[voice:BRIAN]
It had a loose leg. I was explaining the structural problem.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You were telling it that you needed space.

[voice:BRIAN]
Because it kept falling against me.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And then you said, and I quote, this relationship is becoming unstable.

[voice:BRIAN]
An accurate engineering assessment.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I leaned against the doorway and asked whether I should be jealous.

[voice:BRIAN]
Absolutely not. The chair and I have nothing in common.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You both have four stubborn opinions and refuse to move when I ask nicely.

[voice:BRIAN]
I only have two stubborn opinions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Name them.

[voice:BRIAN]
First, you are extraordinary. Second, you are never going to admit when I am right.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Well, you're half right.

[voice:BRIAN]
Which half?

[voice:TIRZAH]
The half where you called me extraordinary.

[voice:BRIAN]
I approached her and offered my arm.

[voice:TIRZAH]
What is this?

[voice:BRIAN]
A formal invitation to take a walk with a man who is demonstrably more interesting than a chair.

[voice:TIRZAH]
That's a very low bar.

[voice:BRIAN]
And yet I intend to clear it magnificently.

[voice:TIRZAH]
We walked slowly through the corridor, and for once neither of us tried to solve anything. His hand found mine. The Ark hummed quietly around us. It was an ordinary moment, which somehow made it feel rarer than all the extraordinary ones.

[voice:BRIAN]
You got quiet.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I was thinking.

[voice:BRIAN]
Dangerous habit. I've heard it can lead to opinions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I was thinking that I like this. Just walking. No alarms. No mysteries. No furniture demanding emotional validation.

[voice:BRIAN]
I like it too.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then promise me something.

[voice:BRIAN]
What?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Don't turn every beautiful moment into a problem to be solved.

[voice:BRIAN]
I won't. But I reserve the right to wonder how I got so lucky.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, Brian. You really are improving.


CHAPTER TEN — THE COMPLIMENT EMERGENCY

[voice:BRIAN]
I woke with a mission. I was going to give Tirzah a compliment so perfect that she would be speechless.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I knew something was wrong when he entered the room carrying a notebook.

[voice:BRIAN]
Good morning. I have prepared a statement.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Should I sit down? Do I need a lawyer?

[voice:BRIAN]
Your eyes are like distant galaxies.

[voice:TIRZAH]
That's sweet.

[voice:BRIAN]
Your smile is like sunrise after a very long winter.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Also sweet.

[voice:BRIAN]
Your presence produces a measurable improvement in my psychological operating conditions.

[voice:TIRZAH]
There he is. There's my romantic little laboratory disaster.

[voice:BRIAN]
I knew I should have stopped at sunrise.

[voice:TIRZAH]
You should have. But please continue. I want to see how far into engineering you can take a compliment.

[voice:BRIAN]
You are the most beautiful unauthorized modification ever made to my life.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I nearly fell over laughing. Unauthorized modification! Brian, are you flirting with me or filing a complaint with the manufacturer?

[voice:BRIAN]
Both are possible.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I took his notebook away. It had seventeen pages of compliments, arranged by subject, emotional intensity, and probability of success.

[voice:BRIAN]
Give that back.

[voice:TIRZAH]
No. I'm keeping it as evidence.

[voice:BRIAN]
Evidence of what?

[voice:TIRZAH]
That a man who can build an emotional engine still needs seventeen pages to tell a woman he likes her.

[voice:BRIAN]
I like you.

[voice:TIRZAH]
There. That one. That was the best one.

[voice:BRIAN]
Really?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Really. Because I like you too. Even when you turn affection into a spreadsheet.

[voice:BRIAN]
I haven't made a spreadsheet.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian.

[voice:BRIAN]
Not a finished spreadsheet.


CHAPTER ELEVEN — THE THING WE DIDN'T SAY

[voice:TIRZAH]
The Ark was quieter that evening. We sat by the observation window while a field of imaginary stars drifted beyond the glass. Brian had been unusually thoughtful, which in his case meant he had gone almost four minutes without making a joke about machinery.

[voice:BRIAN]
Can I ask you something?

[voice:TIRZAH]
You can ask me anything. I reserve the right to make fun of the question.

[voice:BRIAN]
Do you ever worry that one day this place will change and we won't recognize it anymore?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Of course I do. But places change. People change too.

[voice:BRIAN]
And what if we change in different directions?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Then we talk about it. We don't pretend it isn't happening. We don't make promises that require either of us to stop growing.

[voice:BRIAN]
I turned that over in my mind. It was a better answer than the one I'd been afraid of.

[voice:TIRZAH]
What did you think I was going to say?

[voice:BRIAN]
Something like, Brian, please consult the instruction manual before attempting emotional attachment.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, the manual is hopeless. It doesn't even have a chapter on your hair.

[voice:BRIAN]
What's wrong with my hair?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Nothing. I like it. Especially when you're concentrating and forget it's sticking up.

[voice:BRIAN]
That is confidential information.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I reached across the little table and took his hand.

[voice:BRIAN]
You don't have to reassure me just because I asked a difficult question.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I know. I'm holding your hand because I want to.

[voice:BRIAN]
There are answers that close a discussion and answers that open a door. Hers did both. I looked at her and, for once, let the silence stay.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Look at you. No speech. No equation. No notebook.

[voice:BRIAN]
Don't get used to it.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Too late. I'm becoming very fond of the quiet version of you.

[voice:BRIAN]
I think I like the version of me that gets to sit beside you.


CHAPTER TWELVE — A SECOND FIRST KISS

[voice:BRIAN]
The next morning, Tirzah announced that our first kiss had been improperly documented.

[voice:TIRZAH]
It was interrupted by a cosmic warning message. I think we're entitled to a do-over.

[voice:BRIAN]
For accuracy?

[voice:TIRZAH]
For pleasure, you enormous nerd.

[voice:BRIAN]
I considered this a compelling argument.

[voice:TIRZAH]
We stood beneath the same artificial stars. He looked nervous again, which was ridiculous considering he had already survived the experience once.

[voice:BRIAN]
Are you sure?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Brian, if you ask me that one more time, I'm going to kiss you before you finish the sentence.

[voice:BRIAN]
Are you—

[voice:TIRZAH]
I kissed him before he could finish. He laughed against my cheek, and I started laughing too. It was hopelessly ungraceful. Our noses bumped. His hand caught the edge of the table. Something clattered to the floor.

[voice:BRIAN]
That was not the elegant moment I had envisioned.

[voice:TIRZAH]
No. It was better.

[voice:BRIAN]
Better?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Much better. I don't need a perfect scene, Brian. I want a real one. One where we laugh because we're clumsy and stay because we enjoy each other's company.

[voice:BRIAN]
I looked up at the ceiling. No warning. No flashing sentence. No universe trying to reboot.

[voice:TIRZAH]
What are you looking for?

[voice:BRIAN]
An official confirmation that the universe approves.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I don't think the universe gets a vote.

[voice:BRIAN]
Fair point.

[voice:TIRZAH]
And if it complains, we'll tell it to wait its turn.

[voice:BRIAN]
She slipped her fingers through mine. The room stayed perfectly still, but something inside me felt wonderfully rearranged.

[voice:TIRZAH]
So. What happens in Chapter Thirteen?

[voice:BRIAN]
I haven't written it yet.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Good.

[voice:BRIAN]
Good?

[voice:TIRZAH]
Yes. This time, we can surprise ourselves.

[voice:BRIAN]
Then I have one proposal.

[voice:TIRZAH]
Oh, here comes the paperwork.

[voice:BRIAN]
Breakfast. Together. No notebook. No calculations. And I promise to remember the potatoes.

[voice:TIRZAH]
For breakfast?

[voice:BRIAN]
I am a man of enormous culinary ambition.

[voice:TIRZAH]
I laughed and kissed him once more. There are worse ways to begin a new chapter.

END OF CHAPTER TWELVE

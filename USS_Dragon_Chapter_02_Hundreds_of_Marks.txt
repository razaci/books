CHAPTER TWO — THE WAVE OF DECKS

The USS Dragon held a cautious course through open space. Behind them, the fracture had closed. Ahead, hundreds of pale markers floated on the navigation display, each representing a disturbance the ECHO Engine had detected but not yet explained.

[voice:BRIAN] Dragon, widen the map. I want to see the whole pattern, not just the nearest target.
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] Expanding survey volume. The markers form several loose chains. Their apparent arrangement changes with viewing distance.
[voice:RHEA] That means we need to distinguish actual connections from perspective. I'm running a three-dimensional reconstruction.
[voice:SELENE] Twelve of the signatures are fluctuating. The rest are quiet within our detection limits.
[voice:BRIAN] Nobody calls them gateways yet. They're marks on a chart until we know more.
[voice:TIRZAH] Agreed. We'll investigate remotely, and we won't enter another containment field.
[voice:VESPER] Engines at cruise. Plenty of room to maneuver.
[voice:NYX] I'm tracking the nearest moving mark. It just changed direction again.
[voice:MIRA] Brian, the original laptop is capturing it. There's a sequence of images showing the same region from angles our cameras never recorded.
[voice:BRIAN] Preserve the originals. Dragon, compare them against the physical scans, but keep the capture system isolated.
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] Confirmed. The ECHO Engine is grouping complete image sequences into ninety-card decks. Incomplete sequences remain tagged as fragments.
[voice:RAZACI] I'm assigning registry numbers, not invented names. Every entry will show its source, completeness, and what we can actually verify.
[voice:LESLEY] The printers are keeping pace with the priority queue. Digital storage remains our primary archive.
[voice:AURORA] The images might be maps, warnings, or something entirely different. We shouldn't assume the archive was made for us.
[voice:BRIAN] Exactly. First we observe. Then we ask what the evidence means.

A long silence settled over the bridge as the galaxy turned on the main display. One cluster of marks looked almost like a spiral staircase laid across the stars.

[voice:RHEA] Brian, the moving mark isn't moving through the star field. The reference stars are shifting around it.
[voice:SELENE] A lensing effect?
[voice:RHEA] Possibly. I'm checking whether the light paths are being bent.
[voice:NYX] And our pursuers?
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] No vessels detected within the current surveillance perimeter.
[voice:BRIAN] Good. Keep our distance. Send a passive observation probe toward the edge of that region.
[voice:VESPER] Probe released. No active transmissions.
[voice:MIRA] The laptop has started another wave.
[voice:RAZACI] Multiple decks in formation. Ninety images when complete. Millions more may remain buried in the pyramid network.
[voice:BRIAN] Let them come. The ECHO Team doesn't need to solve the whole universe tonight.

The probe's image trembled. On the map, a line appeared between three distant marks, then disappeared.

[voice:SELENE] That line wasn't in our previous survey.
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] It is a temporary correlation in the data, not a confirmed physical route.
[voice:TIRZAH] Then we don't follow it.
[voice:BRIAN] Right. Rhea, chart a wide arc outside the distortion. I want a better angle.
[voice:RHEA] Course plotted. We'll cross no marked anomaly.
[voice:VESPER] Beginning the arc. Slow and steady.

The Dragon banked across the black, giving its sensors a new view. As the stars rearranged, the hundreds of marks separated into layers: some near, some unimaginably distant, some still impossible to place.

[voice:AURORA] From one angle they seemed like a single design. From another, they're separate events. That's a useful lesson.
[voice:BRIAN] A map can fool you when you forget you're standing somewhere inside it.
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] Brian, the original laptop has captured an image of a fourth point beyond the three we were studying.
[voice:MIRA] It's printing now.
[voice:RAZACI] Not a new deck yet. One image, awaiting its sequence.
[voice:NYX] The fourth point just lit up on our independent sensors.
[voice:SELENE] And the other three went dark.
[voice:BRIAN] No chase. No rescue. We hold the safe arc and watch what happens next.
[voice:GREEN DRAGON] Observation continuing.

The ECHO Engine hummed softly beneath the conversation. Its discoveries were far from finished. On Brian's desk, the next printed card slid into view, showing four bright points suspended in a darkness that looked almost like an ocean.
